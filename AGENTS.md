# Particle Art Direction (2026-10-04)

Avoid visually frozen matter, uniform brightness, and uniformly sized particles.
Keep particle identity stable while varying light, color, size, and bounded flow
over time. Architectural form may stay calm; the light composing it must feel
alive. Use independent smooth shimmer and coherent currents, not synchronized
noise or a blanket Bloom increase. Preserve dark breathing room and readable
contours. Gameplay telegraphs and projectile patterns may favor uniformity when
that improves readability. Perceived change in actual rendering matters more
than merely having a time-dependent formula.

and emphasize continuous momentum and delayed follow-through rather than
replacing every movement with a complicated skeletal or fluid simulation.
